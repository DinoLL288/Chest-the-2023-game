Rules Reference

Pieces and HP
  • Prawns x9: 1 HP. Move 1 square forward. From their starting prawn row, they may move 2 squares forward if both squares are empty.
  • Castles x2: 3 HP. Move any distance in any visible straight or diagonal line until blocked. Capture by landing on an enemy.
  • Horseys x3: 2 HP. Move diagonally any distance and may jump over pieces. They only capture by landing on an adjacent diagonal enemy unless using charge.
  • King x1: 5 HP. Normally only moves when capturing 1 square in any direction. When in chest, it can move 1 square in any direction.
  • Queen x1: 3 HP. Cannot normally capture. Moves 1 square diagonally and can be protected by ordered prawns.
  • Staff x2: 1 HP. Moves up to 5 squares vertically or horizontally and captures by landing on enemies.
Core Battle Rules
  • White goes first. Turns alternate after every move or ability.
  • Pieces have HP. Most attacks deal 1 damage unless boosted or using a special attack.
  • A piece is captured when its HP reaches 0. Captured pieces appear in the captured tray.
  • Capturing the enemy king ends the match.
  • Chest currently means the king has an enemy next to it. While in chest, the king gets normal 1-square movement.
Prawn Specials
  • Jump capture: A prawn captures like checkers by jumping over a forward-diagonal enemy into an empty square beyond it.
  • The Dash: When a prawn reaches the far end, it dashes back to its starting prawn row in the same file.
  • During The Dash, enemy pieces in that file take 1 damage.
Castle Specials
  • The Rooky: A king can swap places with a friendly castle up to 5 times per match.
  • The Rooky cannot be used while that king is in chest.
  • Insta movement: After move 10, each castle can once move to any empty square on the board.
Horsey Specials
  • The Charge: A horsey chooses a diagonal direction. Each charge adds 3 squares of range.
  • The first charge locks the direction. Releasing the charge attacks along that diagonal and can eliminate pieces in the path.
  • Each horsey can release charge twice per match.
  • The What?: Once per match, each horsey can move to any empty square of the opposite colour.
Staff and Royal Rules
  • A king or queen can pick up a friendly adjacent staff.
  • While holding a staff, a king or queen may also move like the staff: up to 5 squares vertically or horizontally.
  • A held staff can be ejected to an adjacent empty square.
  • If a king or queen holding a staff is attacked, the staff breaks and is no longer held.
  • White staff can strengthen a friendly piece for 2 turns, making its attacks deal 2 damage.
  • Black staff can heal an adjacent friendly piece by 1 HP.
  • Black staff can absorb an adjacent enemy up to 5 times per staff, capturing it and borrowing that piece's movement for 2 turns.
White Abilities
  • White jump: White can grant a friendly non-king piece a one-use jump over one piece. This can be used 5 times per match.
  • Queen orders protection: White queen can choose the king or queen to protect. The closest 3 friendly prawns become shields. This can be used 2 times per match.
  • Shield prawns take damage for the protected royal piece until they are destroyed or protection is reassigned.
  • Royal teleport: While holding a staff, white king can teleport to any empty square 3 times; white queen can do it 2 times.
  • Staff blast: While holding a staff, white king can blast up to 5 squares forward once; white queen can blast up to 3 squares forward once.
  • Queen of Hungary: White queen can move backward to any empty square 3 times per match.
Black Abilities
  • Invisibility: Black can make one non-king piece invisible for 2 turns, 2 times per match.
  • An invisible piece dodges attacks from non-kings and can only move 1 square on its next move.
  • Queen revive: Black queen can revive a captured black piece by targeting its death square. The revived piece appears on an empty square next to the queen. This can be used 5 times per match.
  • Time freeze: Black king can freeze an adjacent enemy for 3 turns once per match. Black queen can freeze an adjacent enemy for 2 turns once per match.
